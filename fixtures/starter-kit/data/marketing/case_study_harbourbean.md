---
title: Case study — Harbourbean Coffee rebrand
sensitivity: public
---
Harbourbean Coffee opened its second outlet in Tiong Bahru in 2025 and needed a
brand that would work on a cup, a shopfront and an app icon. Lumen Studio
delivered a new wordmark, a four-colour palette and a packaging system in five
weeks. Approved for publication by Harbourbean on 12 Mar 2026.

Results reported by the client: 31% more Instagram follows in the first month,
and the packaging was featured in a local design roundup.
