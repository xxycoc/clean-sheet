Lumen Studio is a Singapore design studio for small businesses that need to look
bigger than they are. Brand identity, websites, pitch decks and launch campaigns,
delivered by one designer who answers her own emails.

Founded 2022. Typical project: two to six weeks. Typical client: a founder who
has just raised money, hired their first employee, or opened their first shop.

Services
- Brand identity: logo, colour, type, brand guide
- Websites: design and build on Webflow or Framer
- Decks and documents: pitch decks, sales decks, annual reports
- Launch campaigns: social templates, email templates, event collateral
